SyncAudio - play music on several Macs at once, in sync.

INSTALL
1. Drag SyncAudio onto the Applications folder.
2. Open SyncAudio from Applications.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
macOS may say it "can't verify" SyncAudio, because it isn't from the
App Store. To open it anyway:
  1. Click "Done" (or "OK") in that message.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to SyncAudio, then confirm.
You only have to do this once.

When SyncAudio asks:
  - "find devices on your local network"  -> Allow (needed to find the other Macs)
  - "access the microphone"                -> Allow (only on the Mac that shares;
                                              it listens to BlackHole, not your mic)

USING IT
- Mac playing the music: click "Start sharing". The first time it offers to
  install BlackHole, a free audio driver; click Install and type your password.
- Every other Mac: open SyncAudio and click "Join" next to the sharing Mac.
- Any number of Macs can join. All of them need to be on the same Wi-Fi.
